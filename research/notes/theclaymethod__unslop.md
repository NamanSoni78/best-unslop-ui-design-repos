- What it is: A prose de-AI tool shipped as an agent skill plus standalone Python scanners: four commands (teach/cleanup/rewrite/mimic) covering detection of formulaic writing, targeted repair, and writing in a taught personal voice. Its distinguishing feature is unusual benchmark honesty: the README's headline product claim is currently a **no-ship**.
- Scope: prose (English only — non-English input is detected and declined, README.md:190-191).
- Key files read: README.md (250 of 512 lines, first half incl. benchmarks, detection layers, protections, voice); SKILL.md (38 lines, full); evals/CORE-RESULTS.md (head + v9 section); scripts/ listing; references/ and presets/ listings.
- Mechanics:
  - Four commands: `/unslop teach` (build a voice profile from your own writing), `/unslop cleanup` (flag-only suggestions, changes nothing), `/unslop rewrite` (diagnose + rebuild under guards, the default), `/unslop mimic` (draft/rewrite in a taught voice, must still clear every removal gate — "Voice never buys an exemption from the constitution", README.md:234).
  - Three detection layers, all deterministic Python (stdlib-only, Python 3.8+): phrase layer (16 literal triggers with contextual false-positive protection; quotes/blockquotes/code fences masked so docs teaching bad writing don't self-flag); structure layer (36 structural patterns + document metrics: sentence_burstiness, triad_density, bold_colon_listicle_count, signpost_density, conclusion_coda, with genre carve-outs via `--genre docs`/`--genre social`); silhouette layer (arrangement-level: scaffold_opener_share, callback_content — described as "the strongest single tell", role_entropy_bits, preview_fulfillment, heading_preview, composite silhouette_penalty flagged at 1.0 against a committed human reference; on the repo corpus "12 of 12 AI documents flagged and 0 of 8 human documents flagged", README.md:111-112).
  - Removal catalog organized by families (throat-clearing openers, emphasis crutches, significance inflation, negative parallelism, wh-opener self-Q&A, cliffhanger fragments, vague attribution, reader flattery, business jargon collocations, chatbot residue, em-dash overuse "Default is zero; two or more in one paragraph is always a hard flag", README.md:157).
  - Preservation is "half the product": register guards for legal/medical/security/scientific hedges, literal domain usage, quoted examples, facts with magnitude-aware checking ("$47.3M can't silently become $47.3 billion", README.md:187), genre carve-outs, non-English refusal.
  - Voice machinery: harvest adapters for Claude Code and Codex transcripts that strip assistant/developer turns to avoid contaminating the profile with assistant-authored text; stylometric profile (3-grams, function-word deltas, MTLD, impostor z-scores, GI rank); voice card; A/B calibration game; hill-climbing refine with a gaming-resistant composite and a divergence guard that raises `reward_hacking_warning` when pool scores improve while held-out worsens.
  - Benchmark honesty (the briefed item, confirmed): README.md:26-29: "The latest independent public result is a **no-ship**: UNSLOP materially improved recall and repair over plain Luna, but it did not yet meet the precision, damage, or whole-document safety bar." evals/CORE-RESULTS.md:142-151 (v9): the skill arm found/repaired more annotated issues than a neutral prompt, but produced more false positives, caused one real damaging edit, and left every dirty document short of safe whole-document improvement; "The sealed holdback was not opened after this public failure." Results are split into three scoreboards (core product / voice / engineering) "so supporting machinery cannot inflate the product result" (README.md:32-34). Core-Results also discloses a V7 benchmark-construction failure and scorer normalization corrections after outputs existed — unusual candor about methodology.
- 3 non-obvious ideas worth adopting:
  1. The no-ship discipline itself: publish paired with/without-skill arms, freeze thresholds before runs, keep a sealed holdback, and let the README's headline be the failure — the single most credible quality signal in this whole batch.
  2. Silhouette-layer metrics (callback_content, scaffold_opener_share, role_entropy_bits) — detecting AI arrangement, not just AI vocabulary; the layer above surface tells that word-ban approaches cannot see.
  3. Magnitude-aware fact preservation and contamination-guarded voice harvesting (dropping assistant turns before profiling) — two failure modes of naive de-AI tools solved explicitly.
